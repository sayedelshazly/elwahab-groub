## Overall Impression

The group homepage has a clear entry point and a coherent industrial look. Trust drops when visitors reach factory pages: the specific capabilities and claims aren’t backed by enough product, facility, or certification evidence. The biggest opportunity is to make every factory page prove what that factory builds and make it clear how a buyer reaches the right team.

## What’s Working

- The homepage introduces three subsidiaries with concise summaries and direct links, giving visitors an easy first routing choice.
- Shared typography, palette, navigation, and CTA patterns create a recognizable group identity across pages.
- Product categories lead naturally toward contact, and the page structure provides a clear progression from group overview to subsidiary details.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Factory pages need factory-specific proof.**  
**Why it matters:** The shared page pattern and broad category imagery make it hard for fleet buyers to tell what each factory can actually deliver.  
**Fix:** Add real facility, process, team, and product evidence for each subsidiary. Label illustrative photos wherever they appear.  
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Claims need supporting evidence or narrower wording.**  
**Why it matters:** Statements such as CMC’s “sole producer of GM truck beds in Egypt for decades,” Helwan’s armored-vehicle and safety claims, and Interbus’s performance claims are difficult for a buyer to verify from these pages.  
**Fix:** Link relevant dated specifications, certifications, or case studies; otherwise make the claims more specific and supportable.  
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

**[P1] Contact routes don’t show factory-specific ownership.**  
**Why it matters:** Factory pages send visitors to the same group WhatsApp, phone, and email endpoints without stating how inquiries are routed. A buyer may not know whether the right team will receive the request.  
**Fix:** Explain the shared routing and add a factory-specific contact destination or a clear factory selector.  
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Product cards look like paths to detail, but don’t lead anywhere.**  
**Why it matters:** Card imagery and labels suggest visitors can inspect a model, yet product cards have no specifications, links, or inquiry action.  
**Fix:** Make each card link to available model information or a product-specific inquiry; identify sample imagery in the card itself.  
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable shape`

**[P2] Repeated slogans obscure concrete value.**  
**Why it matters:** Phrases such as “Built to last,” “Built together,” and “Move forward” repeat broad promises, making factory pages slower to scan and less distinct.  
**Fix:** Replace repeated slogans with short, subsidiary-specific capability statements.  
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`
