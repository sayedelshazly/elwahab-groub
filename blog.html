01 / SPECIALIST VEHICLES

HELWAN BODY BUILDERS

BUILT AROUND
THE WORK.

Every specialist vehicle starts with a clear job to do. Helwan Body Builders creates custom vehicle bodies shaped around how each customer plans to use the vehicle.

From armored and service vehicles to moving stores and caravans, different applications call for different layouts and details. The goal is a practical vehicle that is ready for the work ahead.

02 / PASSENGER MOBILITY

INTERNATIONAL COMPANY FOR BUSES

MOVING PEOPLE
WITH PURPOSE.

Passenger transport has to fit the journey. International Company for Buses brings together micro, mini and midi bus options, with seating capacities ranging from 14 to 34.

That range gives operators room to consider the route, passenger needs and the kind of vehicle that fits their service. It is one part of the group’s wider experience in bus manufacturing.

03 / COMMERCIAL TRANSPORT

COMMERCIAL MOTORS COMPANY

COMMERCIAL BODIES,
BUILT FOR BUSINESS.

A commercial vehicle body is part of the working tool. Its design needs to suit what the vehicle carries and how it is used day to day.

Commercial Motors Company brings decades of truck-bed production experience to its work with partners, including GM Egypt. That manufacturing focus is central to the company’s role in commercial transport.
